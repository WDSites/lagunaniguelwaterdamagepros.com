Pick the stage of your water loss

A water loss moves through stages: water comes out, the structure dries, wet materials are cleaned up, and damage is rebuilt. Mold becomes a separate job when moisture sat too long. Each service page explains one stage in depth, with local conditions, cost factors and the questions to ask.

Who does what in a Laguna Niguel water loss

Not every problem belongs to a restoration company. A leaking pipe needs a plumber to repair it, a roof leak needs a roofer, and a soaked ceiling in a condo may involve your association. We handle the water, the drying and the cleanup, and we say so when another trade should come first.

ProblemWho fixes the causeWho dries and restores
Burst pipe or failed supply lineA licensed plumberWater removal, mitigation and cleanup
Roof or window leakA roofer or a window contractorMitigation, then repair of drywall and paint
Sewer backupA plumber for the private lateral, or the sewer agency for the public lineFlood damage restoration with safety steps
Water entering through a foundation or retaining wallA waterproofing or geotechnical specialistCleanup and drying of what already got wet
Leak from an upstairs unitThe responsible owner or associationMitigation and repair of the unit below

Restoration is not waterproofing. If the cause is still active, fixing it comes first.

Before any work begins

Every job starts with the same promise: we explain the scope and the price before work starts. Ask any restoration company whether its crews work to ANSI/IICRC S500 for water damage and S520 for mold, and ask to see proof of license and insurance. Contractors State License Board rules require a license for work of $1,000 or more in labor and materials combined, and you can check any license on cslb.ca.gov.
