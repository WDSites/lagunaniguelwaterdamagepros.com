Standing Water in Your Home Right Now: Safety Comes First

Before you touch the water

  • ✓If water is touching outlets, a panel, or an appliance plugged in at floor level, stay out. Call 911 or your electric utility (Southern California Edison or San Diego Gas & Electric, depending on your address) before you call us.
  • ✓If you smell gas, leave the house and call SoCalGas, then 911. Do not flip switches on the way out.
  • ✓Cut power only if the panel is dry and you can reach it without stepping in water.
  • ✓Treat water from a toilet, drain or the outdoors as contaminated. Keep children and pets away from it.
  • ✓Do not run a household vacuum or any plugged-in tool in standing water.
  • ✓Photograph the water line and damaged items before anything is moved.

After that, call the line. Nights, weekends and holidays are handled the same way as weekday calls, with same-day response when available. We explain scope and price before work starts, so you know what you are agreeing to while the pumps are still being set up.

When Water Is Past the Point of Mopping

Water above the baseboards

Depth means water is already wicking up the wall and soaking padding. Towels and a shop vacuum cannot keep up with it.

Carpet that squishes

Padding holds gallons. Surface drying leaves the pad and the subfloor under it wet for days.

Water still arriving

A supply line, water heater or washer hose can keep adding water. Stop the source first, then call.

A bulging or dripping ceiling

Water has pooled above you. Stay out from under it and keep the room closed until it has been assessed.

How the Water Comes Out

1

Assess the water and the hazards

Crews check for electrical risk, identify the water category and use moisture meters and thermal imaging to see where it traveled beyond the visible puddle.

2

Pump the deep water

Submersible pumps and discharge hoses remove standing depth fast, which is the biggest single step toward stopping further soak-in.

3

Extract what the floor still holds

Extraction units with wands pull water out of carpet and padding. Saturated padding is usually cut out and removed rather than saved.

4

Hand off to drying

Air movers and LGR dehumidifiers take over, because EPA guidance is to dry water-damaged areas within 24 to 48 hours to prevent mold.

Burst Pipe Water Damage

A burst pipe moves more water than most people expect, and the first job is the shut-off, not the cleanup. Moulton Niguel Water District says the house shut-off valve is typically at the front of your home just below the hose bib. Close it fully, and if it is stiff or corroded, do not force it. MNWD tells customers to call a plumber.

A burst line under a sink is quicker to contain. Close the small angle stop valves on the supply lines inside the cabinet. Toilets have their own angle stop below the tank. If the flow stops, the damage is usually limited to that cabinet, the wall behind it and the flooring beside it.

Check your water meter afterward. With everything off, any movement means a leak, and movement with the house valve closed can point to the service line, which calls for a licensed plumber or leak detection. We handle water removal and drying. The pipe repair itself belongs to a plumber, and calling one early keeps the water from returning.

Water in the Crawl Space

Many Laguna Niguel homes sit on slab or raised foundations, so a crawl space is the hidden spot where water collects unseen. Pumping it out is routine, but wiring, ducts and insulation up there mean the space should be assessed before anyone crawls in. Do not enter a crawl space holding water near electrical wiring.

Once the water is out, the framing and subfloor need drying and the source needs finding, whether that is a supply leak, a drain or runoff. If the water came from rain or groundwater, our flood damage restoration page covers that case. Stopping runoff from reaching the foundation again is drainage work for a licensed contractor, not restoration.

Rain, Shut-Offs and Utilities in Laguna Niguel

The city's hazard plan puts average rainfall near 13.1 inches a year but calls it feast or famine, with winter storms the main driver. For you, that means finding your shut-off valve and clearing the area around it before winter rather than during the first heavy storm.
