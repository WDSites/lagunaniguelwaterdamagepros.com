A slab leak is a plumbing problem under the floor. The water damage above it is ours to dry. Here is how the two fit together.

A slab leak is a leak in a water or drain line that runs in, under or through the concrete floor slab. You cannot see it, and it is usually found because of a symptom. The plumber locates and repairs the line. We handle the flooring, baseboards and walls that the water reached, once the source is stopped.

Not every Laguna Niguel home sits on a slab. Some homes, especially on hillside lots, have raised foundations with a crawl space. Confirm your foundation type from your plans or ask a plumber. If you have a crawl space, see crawl space water damage instead.

Signs of a Slab Leak

Damp carpet or lifting flooringMoisture is coming up through the slabPhotograph and move belongings

These signs do not prove a slab leak. Roof, plumbing in walls and drains can look the same.

Slab Leak Safety Steps While Water Is Spreading

Work through these steps carefully

  • ✓Close the house shut-off valve. Moulton Niguel Water District says it is typically at the front of the home below the hose bib.
  • ✓Watch the meter. If it keeps moving with the valve closed, the leak is between the meter and the valve, and a plumber needs to know.
  • ✓Smell for gas. Gas lines can also run under a slab. At the first smell of gas, leave the house and call 911.
  • ✓Keep out of water near outlets. If water reaches the panel, call 911 or your electric utility.
  • ✓Pick up rugs and small furniture so the floor can be checked.

How the Plumber and the Restoration Crew Share the Job

1

Plumber finds and fixes the leak

Leak detection pinpoints the line, and the plumber repairs it by cutting the slab, rerouting the line or replacing pipe.

2

We map the moisture

A moisture meter and thermal camera show how far the water traveled in flooring, walls and baseboards.

3

We remove and dry

Wet carpet, pad and damaged wall bottoms may come out. Air movers and dehumidifiers dry the rest.

4

We verify

Readings are tracked against ANSI/IICRC S500 as a published reference, and equipment comes out when materials are dry.

Cost Factors

Slab leak costs come from two places. The plumber prices detection and the repair, and we price the drying and cleanup. Our price follows how much flooring and wall area is wet, how long the leak ran and how many days of equipment are needed. Scope and price are explained up front, before work starts.

Insurance Fit for Slab Leaks

Insurers often draw a line between sudden damage and slow leaks, and slab leaks are often slow. Coverage varies by policy, and we cannot say what will be paid. Ask your insurer whether the cost of access to the pipe is treated separately.

Keep the plumber's leak report and photos. After you send proof of claim, California rules give the insurer 40 calendar days to accept or deny it, and 15 calendar days to acknowledge it in the first place.

Mistakes to Avoid

Ignoring a high bill

A leak that runs quietly raises the bill and the risk of mold.

Drying before the repair

If the line is still leaking, the floor will get wet again.

Replacing flooring first

New flooring over a damp slab can trap moisture and fail.

Assuming it is a roof leak

Stains from below are often plumbing. A plumber finds out.
