Storm water damage is rain that gets into the home. It comes through a roof opening, around a window, under a door, down a wall or across a garage floor. It is different from creek flooding, which is covered on our flood damage restoration page. Here the water usually arrives in smaller amounts, but it can still soak insulation and drywall.

Laguna Niguel's rain is brief and heavy. The city's hazard plan says rainfall has ranged from one-third to more than double normal, so a house that stayed dry for years can take water the first time a storm finds a weak spot.

Storm Safety Steps While Water Is Spreading

Protect people first

  • ✓Stay out from under a bulging or sagging ceiling.
  • ✓Do not touch electrical equipment that is wet, and keep out of water near outlets or the panel. Call 911 or your electric utility if water reaches them.
  • ✓Do not go onto the roof or into the yard during the storm. Wait for conditions to improve.
  • ✓Place towels or sandbags only if you can do it safely, and avoid standing in moving water.
  • ✓Photograph the entry point and every wet room before cleanup begins.
  • ✓Treat water that came across the ground as possibly contaminated and keep children and pets out.

How We Dry a Home After a Storm

1

Find the wet materials

A moisture meter and a thermal camera show which walls, ceilings and floors took on water.

2

Extract and remove

Standing water is extracted. Wet insulation, carpet pad and soaked drywall are removed when they cannot dry in place.

3

Dry the structure

Air movers and dehumidifiers run, and the work follows ANSI/IICRC S500 as a published reference.

4

Clean up

Surfaces touched by runoff are cleaned and sanitized, then readings are checked before equipment is removed.

What Changes the Cost

The main factors are how many rooms took water, whether the water was clean rain or ground water, how much insulation and drywall must come out and how many days of equipment are needed. Several storms in a row can make a repair harder to separate from old damage. The price is explained once the scope is known, and before work starts.

Insurance Fit for Storm Water

Insurers often draw a line between rain that entered through a storm opening and water that rose from the ground. Flood insurance is separate from homeowners coverage. We cannot say what a policy will pay.

Keep the roofer's report, photos of the opening and weather dates. In California the insurer must acknowledge a claim within 15 calendar days and accept or deny it within 40 calendar days of proof of claim.

Prevention and Mistakes

Skipping a pre-season roof check

A roofer can find loose flashing and cracked tiles before the rainy season starts.

Clogged gutters and downspouts

Overflow can run straight down a wall.

Blocking drains with debris

Keep yard and patio drains clear.

Leaving wet insulation in place

It stays wet and can grow mold.
