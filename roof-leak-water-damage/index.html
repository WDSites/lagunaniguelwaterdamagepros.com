A roof leak lets rain reach the attic, the insulation and then the ceiling. By the time a stain shows on the drywall, water has often been moving above it for days. We do not repair roofs. A roofer stops the entry point, and we remove water, dry the structure and clean up what the leak soaked.

Roof Leak Safety Steps While Water Is Still Spreading

Protect people first, belongings second

  • ✓Stay out from under a bulging ceiling. Drywall full of water can drop all at once.
  • ✓Set a bucket or tub under a drip and move furniture and electronics out of the area.
  • ✓Keep the light switch off for any fixture that is wet or dripping, and do not stand in water near outlets.
  • ✓Do not climb onto a wet roof. Let a roofer assess it, and ask for a temporary tarp if rain is coming.
  • ✓Photograph the stain, the drip and the room with the date visible.
  • ✓If a ceiling is already sagging, leave it alone and call. Draining it is a job for a crew with protective gear.

How We Dry a Home After a Roof Leak

1

Trace the water path

Thermal imaging and a moisture meter show how far the water spread across ceilings, walls and insulation.

2

Remove what cannot dry

Wet insulation and sagging drywall are cut out so the framing can dry. Intact ceilings are sometimes dried through small access holes.

3

Set drying equipment

Air movers and dehumidifiers run on the affected level, with the room closed off so the readings are reliable.

4

Track the readings

The work follows ANSI/IICRC S500 as a published reference, and the crew checks readings until the framing is back to dry targets.

Cost Factors and Who Pays for What

The roof repair is one cost and the interior drying is another. The roofer prices the roof. We price the water removal, drying and cleanup after we see the scope.

What moves the interior price is the number of ceilings and walls affected, how much insulation is wet, whether the attic must be dried, and how many days of equipment are needed. Painting and drywall replacement are repair work that follows drying.

Insurance Fit for Roof Leaks

Insurers often separate a storm opening in the roof from a roof that failed from age and wear. The cause matters, and so do maintenance records. We cannot say what a policy will pay.

Keep the roofer's report, photos of the damage and any dates of past roof work. Under California's fair claims regulations an insurer has 15 calendar days to acknowledge a claim and 40 calendar days to accept or deny it once proof of claim is in.

Roof Leak Mistakes and Prevention

Painting over the stain

Paint hides moisture and mold growth. Dry the area first.

Ignoring the attic

Wet insulation in the attic can keep dripping onto the ceiling for weeks.

Waiting for the next storm

A roofer can inspect before the rainy season, which NOAA's climate normals show running from about November to March in this area.

Hiring the wrong trade

Roof work needs a roofer. California's Contractors State License Board lists roofing as its own classification.
