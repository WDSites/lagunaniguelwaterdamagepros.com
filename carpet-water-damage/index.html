Carpet is made of three layers: the face fibers, the backing and the pad underneath. Water soaks the pad first, because it is thick and absorbent, and then it can reach the subfloor. A carpet can look dry on the surface while the pad stays wet below.

Whether carpet is saved or replaced depends on three things: the kind of water, how long it stayed wet and what is under it. We take readings before deciding. We do not promise to save every carpet, and we say so when replacement makes more sense.

Save or Replace: How the Decision Is Made

Water sourceWet timeUsual outcome
Clean supply line waterCaught within a day or twoCarpet often dried in place, pad often replaced

Rain or ground waterAnyOften replaced because it can carry contamination

Readings and the water category guide the call. ANSI/IICRC S500 is the published reference.

Wet Carpet Safety Steps While Water Is Spreading

Do these in order

  • ✓Stop the water source first. For a supply line close the angle stop, or close the house valve, which Moulton Niguel Water District says is typically at the front of the home below the hose bib.
  • ✓Do not use a household vacuum on wet carpet. It can shock you and ruin the motor.
  • ✓Stay out of the water if it is near outlets, power strips or the panel. Call 911 or your electric utility.
  • ✓Lift furniture off the carpet and put foil or plastic under the legs.
  • ✓Take photos of the wet area, the source and any stains before cleanup.

How Wet Carpet Is Handled

1

Test the water and the pad

Moisture meters check the carpet, the pad and the subfloor, and a crew decides what the water category means.

2

Extract

Weighted extraction tools pull water from the fibers and the pad. Floor-level extraction is much faster than a household wet vac.

3

Lift and dry

The carpet may be lifted at an edge and floated on air so it can dry from below, while the pad is removed if it is soaked.

4

Clean and verify

Air movers and dehumidifiers run until readings reach dry targets, and the carpet is cleaned and deodorized.

What Changes the Cost

The factors are how many square feet are wet, whether the pad must be replaced, how long drying takes and whether the carpet or only the pad needs replacement. A clean-water loss caught fast costs less than a contaminated or old one. We confirm the scope, then explain the price, before work starts.

Insurance Fit for Carpet Water

Coverage varies by cause. A sudden leak is often treated differently from a slow one. We cannot say what a policy will pay.

Keep photos of the wet carpet and a sample of any stained pad if the insurer asks. Expect two deadlines under California regulation: 15 calendar days for the insurer to acknowledge the claim and 40 calendar days to accept or deny it after proof of claim.

Mistakes to Avoid

Waiting for it to dry on its own

The EPA says wet materials should be dried within 24 to 48 hours to prevent mold.

Using a shop vacuum and fans alone

The pad underneath stays wet.

Walking on wet carpet

It spreads stains and pushes water into the pad.

Skipping the subfloor

A wet subfloor under dry carpet grows mold.
