Emergency Crawl Space Water Damage in Laguna Niguel, CA

Not every Laguna Niguel home has a crawl space, but when one floods the water stays out of sight. Here is how it is handled.

A crawl space is the shallow area under a raised floor. It exists only in homes built on a raised foundation, so many Laguna Niguel homes do not have one. If yours does, you can usually reach it through a small hatch in a closet, a hallway or the exterior. Water there can come from a leaking pipe, a drain that backs up or rain that finds its way under the house.

The reason it matters is the floor above. Water that stays under a house wets the floor joists, the subfloor and any insulation, and it raises humidity in the space. That moisture can reach the living area through the floor and the walls. A wet crawl space is also hard to judge by looking in.

Many crawl space problems are plumbing. Rain-driven water is the other common source.

Crawl Space Safety Steps: Do Not Go In While Water Is Spreading

Stay out and handle these first

  • ✓Close the house shut-off valve. Moulton Niguel Water District says it is typically at the front of the home below the hose bib.
  • ✓Do not crawl in if there is standing water and wiring or outlets under the house. Electricity and water can be a lethal mix.
  • ✓Watch for gas lines, loose insulation and sharp debris. Crawl spaces are tight and hard to leave in an emergency.
  • ✓A gas odor means everyone leaves the home and someone calls 911 from outside.
  • ✓Call a plumber if you suspect a pipe leak and call us for the water and drying.

How We Remove Water and Dry a Crawl Space

1

Assess from the hatch

The crew looks at the depth, source and condition of the space, and decides what protective gear is needed.

2

Pump out the water

Submersible pumps and extraction equipment remove standing water and wet debris.

3

Remove wet insulation and loose materials

Insulation that has soaked and vapor barrier that has failed are removed so the joists and subfloor can dry.

4

Dry and monitor

Air movers and dehumidifiers dry the space and the subfloor, and readings follow ANSI/IICRC S500 as a published reference.

What Moves the Cost of Crawl Space Work

Access matters most. A tight hatch and a low clearance make the work slower, and the price follows. The other factors are the depth of the water, how much insulation is wet, whether the subfloor above is wet and how many days of equipment are needed. Before work starts, we walk through the scope and the price.

Insurance Fit for Crawl Space Water

Coverage depends on the source. A sudden pipe break is often treated differently from groundwater or runoff, and flood insurance is a separate policy from homeowners insurance. We cannot say what will be paid.

Keep the plumber's report and photos. The California Code of Regulations lets an insurer take 15 calendar days to acknowledge a claim and 40 calendar days to accept or deny it after proof of claim.

Prevention and Mistakes

Ignoring a musty smell

It often means moisture has been under the house for a while.

Closing the vents with water inside

Trapped moisture can raise humidity under the floor.

Leaving a wet vapor barrier

Water can pool on it and sit against the joists.

Treating drainage as a restoration job

If rain keeps entering, a drainage or waterproofing contractor needs to fix the path.
