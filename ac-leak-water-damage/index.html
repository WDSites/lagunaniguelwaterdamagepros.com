AC Leak Water Damage Repair in Laguna Niguel, CA

Air conditioners make water as they run, and a blocked drain sends it into the home. This page covers the drying and cleanup side.

An air conditioner pulls moisture out of the air as it cools, and that water has to leave through a drain line. When the line clogs, the pan overflows, a float switch fails or the coil ices and thaws, the water goes into the ceiling, the wall or the floor beside the unit. We handle the drying and the cleanup of whatever the water touched. An HVAC contractor repairs the system.

This page applies only to homes with an air conditioner. How common that is changes from home to home in Laguna Niguel, so check what yours has. Central units with an attic or closet air handler and wall-mounted ductless units can both leak. A leak from either one can run for days.

Water that comes back every summer usually points to a drain line problem.

AC Leak Safety Steps: Power and Water Do Not Mix

Do these in order

  • ✓Turn the system off at the thermostat so it stops making more water.
  • ✓If you can reach the breaker without stepping in water, switch off the breaker for the air handler as well.
  • ✓Keep out of the attic if it is wet. Wet insulation is heavy and wiring may be nearby.
  • ✓If water is dripping from a light fixture or ceiling vent, leave the switch off and stay clear.
  • ✓Photograph the stain, the unit and the drain pan if you can see it safely, then call for drying and an HVAC contractor for the repair.

How We Dry an AC Leak

1

Trace the water

A thermal camera and moisture meter show how far the water traveled from the unit across insulation and drywall.

2

Remove wet materials

Insulation and softened drywall are removed. Sound framing is left in place to dry.

3

Dry the cavity

Air movers and dehumidifiers run with the area contained, and attic work follows safe access.

4

Confirm

Readings are checked against ANSI/IICRC S500 targets before equipment is removed.

What Changes the Cost

The factors are how long the drain ran, how much insulation is wet, how many rooms are below the unit and whether the attic must be dried. Equipment days also add up. Scope and price are explained up front, before work starts.

Insurance Fit for AC Leaks

A sudden overflow and a slow drip are often treated differently, and maintenance history can matter. Policies vary, and we cannot say what will be paid.

Keep the HVAC contractor's report and photos of the unit. After you send proof of claim, California rules give the insurer 40 calendar days to accept or deny it, and 15 calendar days to acknowledge it in the first place.

Prevention and Mistakes

Skipping drain line service

A yearly check by an HVAC contractor can find a clog before it overflows.

Running the unit with a wet ceiling

It keeps making water.

Painting over a stain

Moisture and mold stay behind the paint.

Ignoring a float switch

A float switch shuts the unit off when the pan fills. If it is missing or stuck, the water has nowhere to stop.
