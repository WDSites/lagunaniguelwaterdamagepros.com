This page covers water removal, drying and cleanup for commercial spaces in Laguna Niguel. The work is plain: we remove water, dry the structure, clean affected areas and document what was done. Repairs and rebuilding are scoped separately once the space is dry.

A commercial loss often involves more than one party. There may be a building owner, a tenant, a property manager and an insurer. The first call should say who controls access, where the water came from and whether other units are affected.

Responsibility depends on your lease or ownership documents. We do not decide it.

Commercial Water Safety Steps for the First Hour

Do these steps if you can do them safely

  • ✓Close the water supply that feeds the leak. If the building has a main shut-off, find it and tell the manager.
  • ✓Keep staff and customers away from wet floors and from areas under a sagging ceiling.
  • ✓Do not touch electrical equipment that is wet. Call 911 or the electric utility if water reaches panels or outlets.
  • ✓Photograph the damage, the source and any wet inventory or equipment.
  • ✓Notify the owner, manager or association and your insurer, then call us.

How We Handle a Commercial Water Loss

1

Scope the loss

A moisture meter and thermal camera map where water went, including under flooring and above ceilings.

2

Remove water and wet materials

Extraction removes standing water. Wet ceiling tile, insulation and carpet are removed when they cannot be dried.

3

Dry the structure

Air movers and dehumidifiers dry the space, and the work follows ANSI/IICRC S500 as a published reference.

4

Document

Photos, moisture logs and a written scope support your insurance discussion.

What Drives the Cost

The factors are the area wet, the building materials, how many floors or units were reached, whether the water was clean or contaminated, and how many days of equipment are needed. Working hours and access matter too. The price is explained once the scope is known, and before work starts.

Insurance and Lease Questions

A commercial policy, a landlord's policy and a tenant's policy can all apply, and each has its own terms. We cannot say what will be paid. Your insurer or your broker can explain it.

In California the insurer must acknowledge a claim within 15 calendar days and accept or deny it within 40 calendar days of proof of claim. Keep photos, invoices and your lease.

Mistakes to Avoid

Waiting for the manager to call back

Water keeps spreading. Shut off what you can and call.

Moving inventory without photos

Photograph it first so the loss is documented.

Drying only the visible area

Wet insulation and flooring layers stay wet out of sight.

Assuming the building owner handles it

Tenant and owner duties differ. Check your lease.
