Dirty Water on the Floor Right Now: Your First Moves

Before cleanup crews can start

Do only what is safe. Anything touching electrical or involving a drain is handled differently.

1

Find out where it came from

A broken supply line is clean water at first. A toilet overflow, washing machine drain or sewer backup is not, and that changes everything about the work.

2

Protect yourself

Wear rubber boots and gloves, and avoid wading through water that came from a drain. Keep bare skin out of it.

3

Move what you can

Lift rugs, books, boxes and small furniture to a dry room. Photograph everything before you move it.

4

Leave soaked porous items alone

Do not run a household vacuum on wet carpet or try to dry padding with a hair dryer.

Safety comes before cleanup

If water is touching outlets or a breaker panel, stay out and call 911 or your electric utility, which is Southern California Edison or San Diego Gas & Electric depending on your address. Cut power only if the panel is dry and safe to reach. If you smell gas, leave and call SoCalGas. For sewage, stop using water and keep everyone out of the affected rooms. Then call our line.

What Gets Removed and What Gets Cleaned

ANSI/IICRC S500 defines Category 1, 2 and 3 water. Category 1 comes from a clean source such as a supply line. Category 2 is gray water, like a dishwasher or washing machine discharge. Category 3 is contaminated, including sewage and any water that has sat long enough to turn bad.

The category drives what leaves the house. The table shows the usual pattern, though a crew decides after testing the actual materials.

Water categoryPorous materialsHard surfaces
Category 1Carpet and padding can often be lifted and dried if caught earlyWiped and dried
Category 2Padding is removed, carpet cleaned or replaced, wet drywall cut outCleaned and sanitized
Category 3Carpet, padding and affected drywall are removed and discardedCleaned, sanitized and checked before rebuild

Ask any company whether its crews work to ANSI/IICRC S500, the published industry standard.

Debris Removal and Controlled Demolition

Removing debris means more than hauling out trash. Crews cut drywall at a clean line above the wet mark, pull baseboards, remove wet insulation and bag soaked padding so nothing drips through the house. Extraction units take up remaining water before surfaces are cleaned.

Cutting out wall material feels drastic, but wet drywall that stays in place can hold moisture for days. The EPA says to dry water-damaged areas within 24 to 48 hours to prevent mold, and materials that cannot meet that window usually come out.

Sanitizing and Deodorizing a Wet Home

After debris is out, surfaces are cleaned with detergent, then treated with an antimicrobial product where the water category calls for it. Spraying a scented product over a wet area only hides the problem, so odor work starts after the source material is gone.

Persistent smells usually come from wet subfloor, insulation or carpet padding. Air scrubbers and thermal fogging may be used for lingering odors, and a moisture meter confirms nothing damp is left behind. If the smell returns after cleaning, it is a sign something stayed wet, and you should call again. A dark patch also means mold remediation may be the next step.

Contents Handling: Keep, Clean or Discard

Salvageable

Hard furniture, glass, metal and sealed items can be cleaned and sanitized.

Questionable

Upholstery, books and papers depend on the water category and how long they sat wet.

Discard

Items soaked in Category 3 water, mattresses and soft goods are normally thrown away.

Document

Photos and a list with brand names help your insurer, so take them before anything leaves the house.

Sewage and Drain Backups in Laguna Niguel

Sewer service in Laguna Niguel is Moulton Niguel Water District or South Coast Water District depending on location. The city says the property owner maintains the private lateral from the house to the public sewer, including the portion under the sidewalk or street. A backup in the home often means a blockage in that lateral, so a licensed plumber is the first call.

Cleanup of a sewage backup is a Category 3 job. Stay out, keep pets away and let trained crews work in protective gear.

Most homes here sit on slabs or raised foundations rather than basements, so water often spreads across a single floor and into wall bases. That makes it important to check where water went under baseboards.

About 6,000 homes in the city were built before 1980, according to the housing element. In older homes, ask a qualified professional about testing before cutting into old drywall or flooring. When water is still standing, emergency water removal comes first, and once the structure is clean, water damage repair rebuilds it.
