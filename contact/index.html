What to tell the dispatcher

Have these ready if you can

  • ✓Your address and ZIP code, so coverage can be confirmed.
  • ✓Where the water came from and whether it has been stopped.
  • ✓Which rooms and materials got wet (carpet, hardwood, drywall, cabinets).
  • ✓Whether the water was clean, from a drain or toilet, or from outside.
  • ✓Whether anyone smelled gas or saw water near electrical outlets or the panel.
  • ✓Your insurance details, if you plan to file a claim. You can also share these later.

If water is touching outlets, the panel or anything electrical, call 911 or your electric utility first. If you smell gas, leave the home and call SoCalGas and 911.
