Privacy Policy

How we handle information when you call us or visit this website.

Call recording notice

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

Information we receive

This website has no forms. When you contact us, you call. During a call we may receive your phone number, your name, your address and the details you give about the damage and your property.

Our website host and standard web tools may record basic technical information such as your browser type and the pages you view.

How we use information

We use call information to answer your questions, confirm coverage, dispatch professionals, explain scope and price, and improve how we answer calls. Call recordings are used for quality and training.

Sharing

To serve you, we share the details needed to complete a job with the licensed and insured professionals who perform it. We may also share information when the law requires it.

Your choices

You can ask us not to continue a call, ask what information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it where the law allows. Contact us at contact@lagunaniguelwaterdamagepros.com or call (949) 670-0939.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy, and the current version is always on this page. Questions go to 27921 La Paz Road, Laguna Niguel, CA 92677, or the email above.
