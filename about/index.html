What this business does

Laguna Niguel Water Damage Pros answers calls about water damage and mold at 27921 La Paz Road and dispatches licensed and insured restoration professionals to homes and condos in Laguna Niguel, Aliso Viejo, Laguna Hills, Dana Point, Mission Viejo and Laguna Beach. The line is open every hour of every day.

We explain the scope and the price before work starts. That promise matters more than any slogan, because most people calling a restoration company have never done it before and are being asked to decide quickly.

What we handle and what we do not

Handled

Water extraction, drying, cleanup, repair of water-damaged drywall, ceilings and floors, flood and sewage recovery, and mold remediation.

Not handled

Anything other than water and mold, equipment rentals, and waterproofing of foundations or retaining walls. We will tell you when another trade is the right call.

Standards we point to

ANSI/IICRC S500 for water damage restoration and S520 for mold remediation are the published industry standards. Ask any company whether its crews work to them.

Health questions

We do not diagnose or make health claims about mold. Questions about symptoms belong to a physician.

How we write the guides on this site

The local facts on these pages come from city, county, district, federal and state sources, such as the City of Laguna Niguel's safety and housing documents, Moulton Niguel Water District, NOAA climate normals and the California Department of Insurance. Where a number could not be confirmed from a source like that, we left it out and told you to check with your city or insurer instead.

We also avoid promises we cannot keep for you. We do not quote an arrival time, we do not advertise no-charge visits, and we do not tell you an insurer will approve a claim. What we can promise is a straight explanation of the scope and the price, a licensed and insured professional doing the work, and a phone that is answered at night, on weekends and on holidays.

Nothing here is legal, insurance or medical advice. Policies differ, and your adjuster, your attorney and your doctor are the right people for those questions.

What happens when you call

A dispatcher answers and asks where the water came from, which rooms are wet and whether it has been stopped. Those answers decide which service page fits: removal, drying, cleanup, repair, flood recovery or mold.

Next the dispatcher confirms your ZIP code and explains the scope and the price before any work starts. Same-day response is offered when crews are available, and we never state a fixed arrival time on the phone or on this site, because traffic, storms and crew availability change every day.

How to check any contractor, including us

California requires a Contractors State License Board license for work of $1,000 or more in labor and materials combined. The board has no separate license class for water damage or mold, so restoration work that involves rebuilding falls under the building trades. You can look up any license, check its status and read its bond and insurance details at cslb.ca.gov.

Ask which published standards the crew follows. For water damage the standard is ANSI/IICRC S500, and for mold it is S520. Ask for the scope in writing, ask how drying will be measured, and compare the price against the scope rather than against a phone quote alone.
