How coverage works

Dispatch depends on crew availability that day, so we offer same-day response when available and never a fixed arrival time. Call from anywhere in these towns and say your ZIP code. The dispatcher confirms coverage on the phone.

Several of these cities are served by more than one water district, and parcel boundaries are not something we can confirm for you. Check your water bill for your district name before you call a utility about a shut-off or a leak.

Reputable crews follow the published ANSI/IICRC S500 standard for water damage restoration, and the S520 standard for mold. You can ask any company which standard guides its drying and containment decisions.
