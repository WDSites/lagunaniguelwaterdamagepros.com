Water problems here follow your neighborhood more than the city as a whole. A lakeside home, a hillside home on the east side and a condo with a neighbor overhead each face different risks.

Lakeside homes and the Oso Creek corridor

Lake Mission Viejo is a private lake of about 124 acres behind an earth-fill dam across the Oso Creek canyon, owned by the Lake Mission Viejo Association. The Oso Creek Trail follows the creek for 5.5 miles. Along either one, runoff and ground water are more realistic worries than a roof leak.

After heavy rain, check low rooms, garages and the base of exterior walls first. Photograph any standing water before you move anything, since your insurer will ask where it came from.

Tract homes from the 1970s and 1980s

About 39 percent of Mission Viejo homes were built from 1970 to 1979 and another 27 percent from 1980 to 1989. At that age, water heaters, supply lines, shut-off valves and washer hookups are the usual failure points.

A yearly look pays off. Check the hoses behind the washer, the valves under sinks and the area around the water heater for stains or soft spots. Gradual leaks are often excluded from coverage, so catching one early matters.

Hillside streets on the east side

The city's hazard plan notes that eastern parts of Mission Viejo have varying degrees of landslide potential, and that heavy rain from October to April on hilly ground concentrates the flood-prone areas. If your lot slopes toward the house, water can enter at a slab edge or lower door.

Stay out of any room where mud has entered. Ask your city building department about retaining walls and grading, because restoration crews dry and clean a structure but do not repair a hillside.

Condos and townhomes with a neighbor above

About 14 percent of Mission Viejo housing is in buildings of five or more units, and another 12 percent is attached. A ceiling stain there often comes from the unit above or a shared line, so your first call may be to your association.

Read your CC&Rs for who owns which pipe, take dated photos, then call us so wet drywall and insulation are assessed before mold can start.
